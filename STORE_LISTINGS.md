# Tide Line store listings

Tide Line is free. It does not sell anything inside the kid screens. Adopt a Whale is a grown-up page and the current button does not charge a card.

## Short description

Follow New York humpback whales, practice photo matching, and take a quiet ocean minute. A grown-up goes with you.

## Age

Designed for ages 7–14, with a grown-up area for parents and teachers. Children do not create accounts.

## Privacy notes for the listing

- No ads.
- No third-party analytics or crash reporting SDK.
- No child email address and no free-text chat.
- Location is off until a grown-up allows a quest check. The app does not save where the child is.
- Public whale spots on the map are rounded.

## Questions to settle before a real payment

These are questions for Apple App Store and Google Play. This repo does not guess the answers.

- What is the donation for, in words a parent can read before paying?
- Is the gift consumable, non-consumable, or a subscription?
- Which organization receives the money?
- How does a parent restore a previous gift on a new phone?
- Can a child reach the payment button from a kid screen?

The in-app Adopt a Whale page repeats this list. The pledge button uses a placeholder provider and does not contact a payment company.
